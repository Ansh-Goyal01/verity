- 2026-10-27 (A4): Calibration method = Laplace approximation (MAP by Levenberg-Marquardt on whitened
  residuals in the unconstrained parameter space; posterior N(u_MAP, (J^T J)^-1) with the prior included).
  Chosen over MCMC because the model is smooth, fits take ~0.1-1 s (fleet experiments and the live API
  stay fast) and the covariance is explicit. Known weakness: skewed posteriors at 1-2 trips.
- 2026-10-27 (A4): Likelihood uses one observation per trip (SoC drop first->last reading): variance =
  1/6 pp^2 (rounding of two whole-percent readings) + (rho_rel x observed drop)^2; rho_rel = 0.06 is a
  relative model-discrepancy allowance chosen on the DEV seed only. It under-covered on held-out vehicles
  (72-81 % inside the nominal 90 % band) and is reported as a known weakness, not re-tuned.
- 2026-10-27 (A4): Fleet pooling = empirical Bayes in the eigenbasis of the average data information; the
  between-vehicle variance is floored at 0.30 x class prior variance (0.05 was overconfident on dev). It did
  not beat the plain class prior with only 2 peer vehicles; both curves are reported.
- 2026-10-27 (A4): E1 baselines: nominal-spec = physics at class prior medians; flat rule = claimed range

**WP3b smarter planner (2026-11-21, Ansh: "make the planner smarter, it rejects feasible trips").** The final GO/NO_GO is still the Monte Carlo confidence against the target; what changed is which plans get scored and how real-world costs are priced.
- Root cause: candidate legs were dropped unless they fit at the 90th-percentile energy under an 80 % charge cap, so feasible chains never reached the Monte Carlo (Delhi -> Jaipur, Nexon EV 45: "under 5 %", no plan).
- Charging to 100 % is allowed (`MAX_CHARGE_PCT`); SoC points above `CHARGE_CAP` (80 %) take `ABOVE_CAP_SLOWDOWN = 2.5` times longer (DC taper; approximate - verify), in proposals, the evaluation and the shown charge minutes.
- Candidate legs: a LENIENT filter (median energy, charging up to 100 %) proposes chains next to the STRICT original one; the two sets are unioned, so no chain the old planner found is lost (test). First legs must fit at the TARGET quantile: nothing can be added at the start.
- Robust variants: every multi-stop chain is also scored with each stop charging enough to skip the next one (an unreported charger ahead becomes a top-up, not a must-work stop). Stops that most runs drive past are marked `if_needed` (insurance stops).
- Off-route chargers: real (non-demo) routes look `WIDE_CORRIDOR_KM = 10` km off the road (demo route: 3 km, unchanged). Detours cost battery (there-and-back road km = 2 x offset x `DETOUR_ROAD_FACTOR` 1.3, approximate - verify, at each run's own Wh/km) and minutes, including a failed attempt.
- Evidence check: "every stale charger at its own 10th percentile at once" (a joint event far rarer than 10 %) is replaced by a credible bound: 24 Beta draws of the stale chargers' P(works) from their posteriors, scored on the same runs, 10th percentile of the plan's confidence. Only computed for a would-be GO.
- Prior-only margin (WP2 option B) 10 -> 5 points: at a 90 % target no uncalibrated car could ever GO (95%+ is the top shown value). Not removed: our own real test shows the bands are too narrow (BMW i3: the 95 % band covered 47 %), so an uncalibrated car keeps a margin until WP5.
- Consistency guard: a GO whose expected battery falls below its own Bingo Line anywhere is released as NO_GO (seen only at the rounding edge, e.g. 88.75 % shown as 90 %).
- Speed: `leg_table` scores all pairs vectorised once per plan for both filters; on dense routes (> 60 candidates) proposals use the best 3 chargers per 5 km (reliability, then power, then detour); backups still come from every charger. Delhi -> Jaipur: 9-33 s -> 3-5 s.
- Results (real route, real car, `is_simulated: false`): Delhi -> Jaipur Nexon EV 45 "under 5 %" -> 90 % (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE only by the prior-only margin); XEV 9E 79 GO 95%+; Pune -> Mumbai and Noida -> Agra GO for every car tried. Simulated demo (Ansh accepted): V07 NO_GO 80 % -> GO 95 % (charges 74 min instead of 38), V12 GO, V19 INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. V07's spoken answer re-recorded (2 paid Sarvam TTS calls).

**WP4 trip options (2026-11-21, Ansh approved the plan).** One scoring pass (`_choose`) feeds every objective; each chosen plan goes through the same `_release` as `POST /api/dispatch` (evidence check, decision, Bingo Line guard).
- Objectives: safest = highest confidence at the Monte Carlo's 2-point resolution, then fewest expected lost minutes; fastest = lowest mean minutes among plans meeting the target (displayed confidence, the GO rule); fewest stops = fewest stops among those, ties by minutes. Nothing meets the target: both fall back to the safest plan, `meets_target: false`.
- Merge rule: one option when the stops AND the charge targets are equal. Same chargers charged to different levels are different plans (time vs confidence) and are both shown.
- `avoid_operators` drops chargers from stops and backups; `preferred_operators` restricts planned stops only (a backup of another operator only adds confidence). Unknown names go to `unsupported_preferences`.
- `arrival_buffer_pct` is the destination reserve (`Ctx.reserve_pct`, default `RESERVE_PCT` = 10), used by the leg filter, charge targets, success and the Bingo Line. The stop arrival floor (`RES_STOP` = 5) is unchanged.
- `max_stops` filters the scored candidates; the no-stop plan is always scored, so a cap that rules out every feasible plan shows the best capped plan with `meets_target: false` and says so.
- Speed: options skip the two re-plan levers (cruise at 80, another vehicle): they re-plan the whole trip, not the option. Measured on the recorded Delhi -> Jaipur route, 1,500 runs, in memory: 2 options 4.5 s warm (6.3 s on a cold service), one `/api/dispatch` 4.1 s.
- Each option has its own seed (`seed + 101 * i`) for its Bingo Line and evidence draws; its plan id hashes the request plus its first objective, which is also what a report-triggered re-plan re-runs.

**WP5 honest bands (2026-11-21, Ansh approved the plan, scikit-learn, India rows only, VED dropped).**
- Calibration set: verified India TRAINING-split rows only (71 trips, 40 source pages), through the planner's own Monte Carlo (`india_eval.predictive_samples`). Each prediction is out of sample: the car's posterior is fitted on other source pages (leave-one-source-out). BMW i3 trips are excluded: their per-trip band is parameter-only (`calibrate.predict_delta_soc`), a different predictive path from the one releases use, and in-sample on its own training trips. VED: Apache-2.0 and records HV battery SoC, but only 3 of its 27 PHEV/EVs are pure EVs (2013 Nissan Leafs, Michigan), no elevation, and extracting .7z needed a new tool; dropped inside the time box, not shown unusable.
- Score: `|observed - median| / H90`, H90 = half-width of the model's own central 90 % band, not the guide's IQR. Widening by an IQR-normalised quantile would mis-scale the band (a calibrated model gets k ~ 1.2 on the IQR scale; k_iqr = 1.18 is reported next to k). k = the ceil((n+1) x 0.9)-th smallest score.
- Never narrowed: k < 1 is reported and `k_applied` = max(1, k). Result: k = 0.95, so releases are unchanged. This does NOT contradict the BMW 47 %: that band had no trip-level noise. Caveat: the calibration predictive integrates unknown context (temperature, AC, passengers as scenarios); a release with that context known is narrower, so k ~ 1 shows the planner is not overconfident on trips like these, not that every release band is calibrated.
- Applied to real cars only (`ForecastConfig.band_scale`, widening each segment's cumulative use around its median); simulated demo vehicles say "model only".
- Learned correction: HistGradientBoosting (shallow, fixed seed) on the energy log-ratio (unit-free; inverse for range tests), features distance, speed band, temperature, AC, passengers, battery kWh, record type (a feature unknown for every training trip is dropped). Cross-fitted by source group (5 folds) for its k (0.87). NOT in releases (`in_releases: false`): on held-out n = 21 it improves MAE slightly with the same 90 % coverage; intervals overlap, so it stays an experiment until it clearly beats physics.
- E7 held-out (n = 21, 11 pages, FINAL): 90 % coverage physics 19/21 (Wilson 71-97 %), pure ML regression 14/21 (45-83 %); MAE consumption 29.9 Wh/km physics, 28.6 corrected, 25.7 regression.
- The prior-only margin (WP2/WP3b, 5 points) stays: k ~ 1 on public reports is not evidence about one uncalibrated car.
