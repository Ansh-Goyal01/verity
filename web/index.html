Will I make it?
An honest answer, before you leave.

Verity treats every EV trip like an airline treats a flight. It issues a dispatch release: GO or NO-GO with a tested confidence level, a charging plan built around chargers that actually work, backup chargers within reach, and a live Bingo Line — the battery level below which the driver must divert.

How a release is made

Physics first, uncertainty honest, chargers distrusted until proven.

  1. 01

    Learn the car

    Bayesian calibration turns each vehicle's logged trips into its real usable capacity and drag, pooled across the fleet.

  2. 02

    Simulate the trip

    2,000 Monte Carlo runs with shared trip-level factors (driver, AC, wind, payload), so long trips are not falsely confident.

  3. 03

    Distrust chargers

    Reports are weighed by source reliability (Dawid–Skene); coordinated fake-report rings are flagged in the graph.

  4. 04

    Release or refuse

    GO, NO-GO, or insufficient evidence — with alternates, the Bingo Line, a point of no return and levers that raise confidence.

    Architecture

    Physics first. Probability honest. Graph-checked.

    Energy model · physics.py

    Froll = Crr · ssurface · m · g · cos θ

    Fgrade = m · g · sin θ

    Faero = ½ · ρair(T) · CdA · (v + vheadwind)²

    Finertia = m · (1 + δ) · a

    Pbatt = Pwheel / ηdrive  or  Pwheel · ηregen

    ΔSoC = 100 · Σ (Pbatt + Paux + Pbase) · Δt / Eusable

    Every term is reported separately, which is why the dispatch console can say “air drag −32 points, AC −9 points”. Calibrated per vehicle: CdA and Crr multipliers, ηdrive, AC coefficient, true usable capacity, driver aggressiveness.

    Why shared uncertainty matters

    Never invent data

    No made-up numbers in code, UI or charts. Offline pages load saved engine outputs, and say so.

    Label simulation

    Everything built from simulated data carries a visible “simulated” badge or title.

    Refuse rather than guess

    When evidence is thin, the release says insufficient evidence and lists what would fix it.

    Held-out trips are sacred

    The test split is fixed once and never used for tuning.

    The graph never decides alone

    Neo4j proposes charging chains; each is scored by the Monte Carlo with backup plans.

    Probabilities come from the model

    Rounded to the nearest 5%, shown with their evidence (“based on 3 reports in 60 days”).
