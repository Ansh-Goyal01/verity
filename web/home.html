Will I make it?

Range apps give you a number. Verity gives every EV trip a dispatch release: GO or NO-GO with a tested confidence level, a backup charger for every stop, and a Bingo Line that tells the driver exactly when to divert. When the evidence is thin, it says so.

    01 · The problem

    A pin on a map is not a working charger.

    0/ 0

    chargers along our Noida → Agra demo corridor have no recent report saying they work. Verity starts every one of them at unknown.

    Charger directory: OpenStreetMap + Open Charge Map, merged within 50 m
    ~1 in 6

    public charging stations in India reported not working, in an industry estimate of the network for 2026.

    Source: MeraEV blog (third-party estimate)
    1 number

    is what trip planners give you: a predicted arrival percentage. Guides tell drivers to keep a 10–15% buffer “just in case” — because nobody says how sure the number is.

    Source: ABRP road-trip guide
    “Unknown”

    is how routing APIs label chargers whose status they cannot see. That is honest — but the plan still treats the stop like it will work.

    Source: Chargetrip API FAQ

    02 · How a release is made

    Four steps from “probably” to a signed release.

    01

    Learn the car

    Every vehicle's logged trips calibrate its true usable battery, drag and AC load — a Bayesian posterior, pooled across the fleet so a new car borrows strength from its siblings.

    calibrate.py · usable kWh with p10–p90
    02

    Simulate 2,000 futures

    A physics energy model runs inside a Monte Carlo. Driver, weather, wind and payload are sampled once per trip, not once per road segment — so long trips are not falsely confident.

    forecast.py · expected battery band + Bingo Line
    03

    Distrust chargers

    Reports are weighted by each source's learned reliability, and coordinated fake-report rings are flagged in the graph. A charger nobody has vouched for stays unknown — and gets a backup.

    trust/ · Dawid–Skene + ring detection + Neo4j
    04

    Release — or refuse

    GO, NO-GO, or insufficient evidence. Same booking, three vehicles, three different answers — each with the levers that would change it.

    dispatch.py · decision, alternates, levers

    05 · Where Verity fits

    Others answer how far. Verity answers how sure.

    ROUTE PLANNERS

    Predict a number

    ABRP · Chargetrip · Statiq · EVJoints · HERE

    Plan stops and predict your arrival percentage, using vehicle, speed, weather and elevation. The output is one number.

    CHARGER MAPS

    Crowdsource a score

    PlugShare · network apps

    Roll recent driver check-ins into a station score. Useful — but only as fresh as the last driver who checked in.

    VERITY

    Sign a release

    Physics + probability + trust graph

    A confidence level tested against held-out trips, a backup priced into every stop, a Bingo Line for the drive — and a refusal when the evidence can't support a promise.

    06 · For the driver

    The plan keeps working after you leave.

    Battery readings come in by keypad or voice. When a planned charger is reported broken, the engine re-plans and the phone says it out loud — in Hindi or English — before the driver passes the point of no return.

      07 · Refuse rather than guess

      Never invent data.Every number on this page is read from saved engine output or experiment files.
      Label simulation.Anything built on simulated worlds says so, right next to it.
